WHO AM I

Former student at École Polytechnique, I completed a double degree with KTH Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm in 2021. In march 2021, I started a Ph.D in symmetric cryptography in the team COSMIQ of Inria Paris, under the supervision of Gaëtan Leurent. There, I mainly worked on boomerang attacks against AES-based ciphers and algebraic attacks against arithmetization-oriented primitives. I also codesigned LeMac, which is the fastest MAC in the literature on modern processors to this day. I defended my PhD on the 27/06/2024.
I am currently working as an expert in the cryptography laboratory of ANSSI, and am still performing research. I am mainly interested in other types of attacks on SPN ciphers, e.g. impossible differential or square attacks, and I am still actively involved in the algebraic cryptanalysis of arithmetization-oriented primitives. Recently, I have started working on the cryptanalysis of weak-PRFs and of multivariate signatures. I also enjoy designing and breaking crypto challenges in CTF competitions, under the pseudonym graniter.
